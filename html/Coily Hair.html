Wavy Hair

1. Moisturize, Moisturize, Moisturize

Use a sulfate-free, moisturizing shampoo to cleanse without stripping. Follow with a rich conditioner to hydrate curls and make detangling easier. Apply a leave-in conditioner to maintain moisture between washes.

2. Gentle Washing Routine

Shampoo no more than once a week to avoid stripping oils. Co-wash (with conditioner) in between to cleanse without drying out.

3. Detangle Carefully

Detangle only when hair is wet and saturated with conditioner. Use fingers or a wide-tooth comb, starting from the ends and working up. Be very gentle—coily strands are prone to breakage.

4. Stretching Styles Prevent Breakage

Shrinkage is normal, but too much can cause tangling and knots. Try styles that stretch the hair, like twist-outs, braid-outs, bantu knots, or African threading. Avoid heavy manipulation when dry.
