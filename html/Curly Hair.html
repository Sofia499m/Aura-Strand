Wavy Hair

1. Hydrate Deeply

Use a sulfate-free, moisturizing shampoo to cleanse without stripping. Follow with a rich conditioner to hydrate curls and make detangling easier. Apply a leave-in conditioner to maintain moisture between washes.

2. Wash Less Often

Shampoo just 1–2 times per week to avoid drying out curls. Co-wash (washing with conditioner only) between shampoos if needed.

3. Detangle Gently

Only detangle when hair is wet and coated with conditioner. Use a wide-tooth comb or your fingers to avoid breakage.

4. Style While Wet or Damp

Apply curl creams, gels, or mousses to soaking wet or damp hair. Scrunch curls upward to encourage definition and bounce.
