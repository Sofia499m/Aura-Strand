Straight Hair

1. choose the right conditioner and shampoo

Use sulfate-free products to avoid stripping natural oils. If your hair gets oily quickly, try a lightweight, clarifying shampoo once a week. Use a moisturizing conditioner, but only on the mid-lengths and ends to avoid flatness.

2. Do not Overwash

Washing two to three times a week is ideal unless your scalp gets very oily. Overwashing can strip oils, making your scalp produce more oil to compensate.

3. Protect From Heat

Always use a heat protectant spray before using flat irons or blow dryers. Even though your hair is already straight, heat tools can help style or smooth it—but overuse can cause dryness and split ends.

4. Regular Trims

Trim every six to eight weeks to keep ends healthy and prevent split ends from moving up the strand.
