Wavy Hair

1. Use Moisturizing, Lightweight Products

Choose sulfate-free shampoos that won’t strip natural oils. Use lightweight conditioners to hydrate without flattening the waves. Look for products with ingredients like aloe vera, glycerin, or panthenol for moisture and definition.

2. Don’t Overwash

Washing 2–3 times a week helps retain natural oils and encourages wave pattern. Overwashing can cause dryness and frizz.

3. Detangle Carefully

Use a wide-tooth comb in the shower with conditioner in your hair. Avoid brushing dry waves—it disrupts the wave pattern and causes frizz.

4. Define Waves with Styling Products

Use light mousse, curl cream, or gel while hair is still wet or damp. Scrunch the product in to enhance wave definition.
